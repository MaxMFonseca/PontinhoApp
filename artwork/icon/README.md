# Pontinho launcher icon

`pontinho-icon-source.png` was generated with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool from the user-supplied four-card artwork. It is the editable high-resolution source retained by the project.

`pontinho-play-store-512.png` is the opaque 512 × 512 Play Store upload asset. The Android app uses a separately padded transparent foreground over the zinc `#18181B` adaptive-icon background.

## Final ImageGen prompt

> Adapt the supplied artwork into a clean, production-ready Android adaptive app icon foreground for Pontinho. Preserve the recognizable fan of exactly four standard playing cards, their diamond, club, heart, and spade symbols, the cream cards, and the energetic orange/yellow burst. Simplify tiny flying shards, keep all essential visual content centered with generous transparent padding for circular, squircle, rounded-square, and teardrop masks, and keep the result crisp at small sizes. Use genuine transparency outside the foreground artwork. Do not add words, letters, numbers, watermarks, borders, tiles, or external shadows.
